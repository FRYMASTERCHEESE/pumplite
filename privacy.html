Privacy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Public blockchain information

Blockchain wallet addresses, token contracts, transaction hashes, balances and other on-chain activity are public by design. When you connect a wallet or submit a transaction, that public information can be read from the relevant network and may remain available permanently.

2. Wallet access

PumpLite asks a compatible wallet for account access only when a feature needs it. Transaction signing and authorization happen through your wallet. PumpLite does not ask for or need your seed phrase or private key.

3. Current frontend tracking

The current PumpLite frontend does not include a PumpLite user-account system, advertising tracker or first-party analytics tracker. The site may still be served through hosting and network providers that receive ordinary technical request information such as an IP address, browser information and request time under their own policies.

4. RPC and network requests

To show live blockchain information, PumpLite sends read requests to configured public RPC services. Wallet software may also communicate with its own providers. Those third parties may process network identifiers and ordinary connection information according to their own privacy practices.

5. Token metadata publishing

If you choose to publish token metadata through PumpLite, the selected image, token name, symbol, description and optional links or banner information are sent to PumpLite's configured metadata authorization/upload service and then published to IPFS. Your public wallet address and wallet-signed authorization are used to authorize the upload. Published IPFS content is intended to be public and may be difficult or impossible to remove everywhere after publication.

6. Information you choose to publish

Do not place passwords, seed phrases, private keys, private contact details or other sensitive personal information in token names, descriptions, metadata, social links or other public blockchain/IPFS content.

7. Third-party links and services

PumpLite links to or interoperates with services such as wallet providers, blockchain explorers, RPC providers, IPFS infrastructure and decentralized exchanges. Their privacy practices are controlled by those services, not by PumpLite.

8. Security

PumpLite uses browser security controls and wallet-signed authorization for sensitive metadata publishing flows, but no internet or blockchain system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Protect your device and wallet and verify every signing request.

9. Changes and contact

This page may be updated when PumpLite's data flows change. Privacy questions can be sent to coreyedge123@gmail.com.
