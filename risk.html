Risk Disclosure

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Tokens can lose all value. PumpLite does not guarantee price, liquidity, buyers, profit or recovery of funds.

1. Smart-contract risk

Smart contracts can contain defects, unexpected economic behavior or vulnerabilities. PumpLite's current Base V2 contracts have automated and adversarial tests, but they have not received an independent third-party smart-contract audit.

2. Wallet and signing risk

A malicious website, browser extension or compromised device can attempt to trick you into signing harmful transactions. Check the network, contract, amount and action shown by your wallet before approving. PumpLite never needs your seed phrase or private key.

3. Token and creator risk

Anyone able to use the permissionless launch flow can create a token. A PumpLite identity/provenance badge is not an investment endorsement. For mintable Base V2 tokens, the creator may add market inventory up to the immutable lifetime maximum until minting is permanently locked.

4. Price and liquidity risk

Small markets and liquidity pools can move sharply from small trades. You may receive materially less than expected if market conditions change before confirmation, although PumpLite uses minimum-output protection for its curve trades. A displayed spot price does not guarantee that a large order can execute at that price.

5. PumpLite curve versus external DEX liquidity

PumpLite bonding-curve backing and external decentralized-exchange liquidity are separate. PLITE's Uniswap V2 PLITE/WETH pool can have a different price and liquidity level from the PumpLite curve. DEX reserves, swaps and fees are not counted as PumpLite curve backing or PumpLite curve volume.

6. MEV, slippage and transaction ordering

Public blockchain transactions may be observed and reordered before confirmation. Other trades can change the market before your transaction executes. Transactions may fail while still consuming network gas.

7. Network and third-party risk

Base, Solana, wallet providers, RPC services, explorers, IPFS infrastructure, Uniswap and other services may experience outages, congestion, incorrect displays, changed policies or security incidents. PumpLite cannot guarantee their availability.

8. Irreversibility and metadata permanence

Confirmed blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. Token metadata published to IPFS or blockchain systems can remain publicly accessible even if a website link later changes or disappears.

9. Regulatory and tax risk

Laws, tax treatment and regulatory requirements for tokens and decentralized trading differ by location and can change. You are responsible for determining the rules and obligations that apply to you.

10. No guaranteed indexing or displayed value

Wallets, explorers and market-data providers decide independently whether and how to index a token or show a price. Creating liquidity does not guarantee that Phantom, BaseScan or another provider will display a token price or market value.

11. Free claims and promotions

Receiving a free token does not promise that the token has or will gain monetary value. Network gas may still apply to a claim or related transaction.

12. Risk control

Use amounts you can afford to lose, verify contract addresses independently, keep wallet recovery information offline and private, and do not approve a transaction you do not understand.
