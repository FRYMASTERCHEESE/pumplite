How the curve works

Buys add native currency and remove coins. Sells return coins and withdraw native currency. Quotes round down. The virtual offset sets starting pricing; it is not money available to withdraw.

No scheduled graduation, liquidity migration, owner withdrawal, or price guarantee. Direct donations are excluded from quotes and cannot be recovered. Rounding can leave locked dust. Other traders may move the price within your slippage limit.
