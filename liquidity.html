Build real liquidity

Two separate, transparent funding routes: back a new PumpLite coin with real SOL or ETH purchases, and optionally supply PLITE + WETH to the existing Uniswap V2 pool on Base.

Nothing funds itself when a coin is created. PumpLite charges a 0% creation fee. No launch tax or share of trading fees is automatically routed to PLITE. This page does not connect a wallet, request signatures, make deposits or promise returns. Any real funding requires your own separate wallet review and approval.

1 · Fund the newly created coin

Every approved buy adds native-asset backing to that coin’s PumpLite bonding curve, subject to protocol fees and market accounting. This is not external DEX liquidity and not an irreversible pool lock.

Base: in PumpLite’s Create coin dialog, enter this amount as the optional first buy. Coin creation and that purchase are separately approved in your wallet.

The original PLITE token has an existing Uniswap V2 pool, separate from the bonding curve. To provide real liquidity, a contributor must hold both PLITE and WETH on Base, and match the pool’s current reserve ratio in Uniswap. Do not send tokens directly to the pair contract.

What cannot happen automatically yet?

PumpLite currently has no audited launch-fee splitter, automated PLITE/WETH liquidity allocator, LP-token lock, cross-chain funding mechanism or permission to take money from creators. A production system that splits launch proceeds to a second asset/pool would need explicit creator consent, fresh contract and security reviews, precise allocations/receipt accounting, refunds/failures handling, user-controlled wallet approvals and a new deployment. No part of this page assumes that infrastructure already exists.

Real on-chain deposits and verified trading activity—not extra accounts, displayed market cap or metadata edits—are the relevant evidence of liquidity. An optional first buy is not a promise of a market price or profitable exit.
